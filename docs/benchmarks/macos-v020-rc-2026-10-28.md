## Retained work

| Workload | Elapsed | Work evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Initial 10,000-node tree | 150.643 ms | 10,001 retained nodes; 20,001 layout visits; 130,183 allocations; 111,489,282 allocated bytes |
| Unchanged root invalidation, 10,000 nodes | 45.911 ms | 10,001 descriptions/reconciliations; zero layout, paint, or composition visits |
| Reuse 10,000-descendant region for a sibling change | 11.624 ms | Three descriptions/reconciliations; one reused subtree; zero layout visits |
| Virtual list: one million logical items, 100 frames | 32.384 ms total | 23 retained nodes |
| 1,200 custom-surface updates | 5.311 ms total | Zero ordinary stage visits or allocations |
| Container-rich frame: 4,000 parent buckets | 189.821 ms | 12,001 logical nodes |

The 10,000-frame idle attempt recorded zero retained-stage work, allocations,
and GPU submissions. Structural churn retained 10,001 nodes over 1,000 frames
without creating or retiring nodes.

The reuse case remains concerning despite its small description/reconcile
counters. The [published older Mac capture](retained-work-macos.md) reported
0.356 ms for this case; it also reported 86.198 ms for initial 10,000-node
construction. The OS change and uncontrolled load prevent attributing these
historical timing differences to Alicorn alone. A matched baseline/candidate
comparison on the current machine is the next investigation step.

## CPU material path

The three samples emitted identical direct-quad and flat-material output:
24,576 vertices at unchanged capacity. Median times were 12.973 ns per surface
for direct quads and 12.320 ns for flat material. The small sample and overlap
do not establish a speedup. All 16 material-budget cases passed across scales
1, 1.25, 1.5, and 2 and the four tested relief heights.
